Beef Pilau with Chicken Stew and Kachumbari

I love this meal because the spiced beef pilau pairs perfectly with rich chicken stew and fresh kachumbari, creating a balance of savoury, spicy, and refreshing flavours.

Ingredients

Beef Pilau

  • 2 cups (400 g) basmati or long-grain rice
  • 500 g beef, cut into cubes
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 4 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons pilau masala
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 5 whole cloves
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 4 cups (about 950 ml) beef stock or water
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt

Chicken Stew

  • 750 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups (350 ml) chicken stock or water
  • 1/4 cup fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Kachumbari

  • 3 medium tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh coriander, chopped
  • Juice of 1 lemon or lime
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Preparation

Beef Pilau

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly with cold water until the water runs clear, then drain and set it aside.
  2. Place the beef in a pot with a little water and boil until tender. Drain the beef, but keep the beef stock for cooking the rice.
  3. Heat the cooking oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add sliced onions and fry until they turn golden brown.
  4. Add the crushed garlic and grated ginger. Stir for about 1 minute until they release their aroma.
  5. Add the pilau masala, cumin, black pepper, cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, and cloves. Stir for about 30 seconds to release the aroma of the spices.
  6. Add the cooked beef and fry it with the onions and spices for about 5 minutes.
  7. Add the quartered potatoes and stir gently until they are coated with the spice sauce.
  8. Add 4 cups of the reserved beef stock or water and the salt. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  9. Add the drained rice and stir gently once to distribute everything evenly.
  10. Reduce the heat, cover the pot, and allow the pilau to simmer until the rice is cooked and the liquid has been absorbed.
  11. Turn off the heat and let the pilau rest, covered, for about 5 minutes. Fluff the rice gently with a fork before serving.
